BP Witness

Your blood pressure number is only as good as how you sat for it.

    1. Get set up

    1. Sit in a chair with a back, next to a table. Put the cuff on your bare upper arm.
    2. Put your laptop or phone about 2 m (6 ft) away, at an angle, so the camera sees your whole body, feet included.

    AI cuff check

    Hold your cuff arm close to the camera, then press the button. AI checks that the cuff is on bare skin, not over a sleeve.

    Things the camera can't see. Check the ones that are true:

    2. Rest quietly

    Sit back, feet flat, arm resting on the table. The timer only counts while you're sitting correctly, still and quiet.

    5:00

    3. Take your reading

    Press the start button on your cuff now. Stay still and silent until it finishes.

    0:00

    4. Show the result to the camera

    Hold the cuff's screen up close to the camera, then press the button. AI reads the numbers for you.

    Your result

    BP Witness checks how a reading was taken. It does not diagnose anything or tell you whether your blood pressure is healthy. Share your readings with your doctor, and call emergency services if you feel unwell.
